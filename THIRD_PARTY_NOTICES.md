## Unicode CLDR and ICU data

`internal/icudata/icu.zip` holds data of ICU 78.3 (CLDR 48.0), exported from the Node.js 24.22.1 binary
by the scripts in `internal/icudata/gen/`, and time zone data of tz 2026e as ICU compiles it. The ICU
algorithms followed in `internal/intl` come from the same project.
